# **Prime Root *Angle* Factorization Matrix**

 
---

 
 
 
 
 

The table below demonstrates how the 8 angles vectoring the prime roots in the Prime Spiral Sieve 
 can be used to factorize and account for all composite numbers in the array. There are eight deterministic sequences linked 
 to the prime roots and their angles (reference [Croft Spiral Sieve](index.html#croftspiral)), 
 each possessing a unique initializing and repeating prime root angle multiplier. The second table (bottom of page) 
 shows the repeating patterns for the prime root angle multipliers.
